## 1. What this is

Provider will launch a simple one-page marketing website for Client, based on the demo Client reviewed, with reasonable edits Client requests before launch (hours, phone, services list, short about text, and contact details).

This is not a custom redesign, online store, booking system, blog, or ongoing advertising campaign unless both parties sign a written add-on.

## 2. What Client gets

1. A live website at a domain Client owns (see section 4).
2. The site content and layout as launched, plus a copy of the site files on request after final payment.
3. Help connecting the domain to hosting.
4. **90 days of Basic Updates** starting on the Launch Date (section 7).

## 3. Price

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Website launch (one-time) | **$597.00 USD** |
| Domain registration (paid by Client to the registrar, typically about $12–$20/year) | Client’s cost |
| Hosting, year 1 | Included |
| Basic Updates, first 90 days | Included |

**Optional after 90 days** (Client may decline):

- Pay-as-you-go Basic Update: **$49** per request
- Care plan: **$19/month**, billed monthly, cancel anytime; covers reasonable Basic Updates

A **Basic Update** means one of: hours, phone, address, services wording, a short about-text change, or replacing one image Client provides. It does not include a redesign, new pages, e-commerce, or custom features.

## 4. Domain ownership (so Client is not locked in)

Client owns the domain name. Provider will **not** register the domain in Provider’s personal account as the owner.

Preferred method: Client creates a registrar account (Google Domains / Squarespace, Namecheap, Cloudflare, or similar). Provider sends step-by-step help. Client pays the registrar directly.

If Client asks Provider to buy the domain on Client’s behalf, Provider will list **Client as the registrant/owner** and hand over login or initiate a transfer within **5 business days after final payment**. Provider will not keep the domain as leverage.

Until final payment, the demo may stay on Provider’s temporary web address.

## 5. Payment (so neither side is stuck)

**Total due to Provider: $597.**

1. **Deposit — $300** due when this agreement is signed. This reserves the launch and covers setup work. The live custom-domain site will not be pointed to Client’s domain until the deposit clears.
2. **Balance — $297** due when Provider shows Client the site live on Client’s domain (or a staging URL using that domain) and Client has **3 business days** to request Basic pre-launch fixes. If Client does not send written objections in those 3 days, the site is accepted.
3. Provider sends the invoice by email. Client pays by {{payment_method}} (recommended: card or bank transfer through Stripe or PayPal **Goods and Services**, not Friends and Family).
4. Provider does not transfer files, registrar access, or DNS control until the balance is paid.
5. If Client pays the deposit and then cancels before launch, Provider keeps the **$300** deposit for work already done and does not charge the balance. Client does not receive the launched site or files.
6. If Provider does not launch the site on Client’s domain within **14 days** after (a) deposit is paid, (b) Client has provided a domain Client owns or approved Provider to buy, and (c) Client has sent any requested launch details (hours, phone, logo if any), Client may cancel in writing and receive a **full refund of all amounts paid to Provider**. Domain fees already paid to a registrar are not refundable by Provider.
7. Chargebacks: Client agrees to contact Provider first and allow **10 days** to fix a legitimate launch problem before filing a dispute. Provider agrees to refund under section 5.6 if launch is not delivered.

## 6. Launch details Client must provide

Within 7 days of signing, Client will confirm or send:

- Legal business name and public phone number
- Hours (or “call for hours”)
- Approval of the domain name
- Any logo or photos Client has the right to use

If Client does not send these, the 14-day launch clock in section 5.6 does not start.

## 7. Launch Date and updates

**Launch Date** is the day the site is first reachable on Client’s domain.

For **90 days after the Launch Date**, Provider will complete reasonable Basic Updates within **5 business days** of a written request (email is enough). Unused update days do not convert to cash. After 90 days, updates stop unless Client chooses the $49 or $19/month option.

Provider may pause updates if any invoice is more than 10 days overdue.

## 8. Content and accuracy

Client is responsible for the truth of hours, prices, licenses, and claims. Provider built the demo from public listings and does not guarantee that listings were complete. Client should check the site before launch.

Client must only send photos and logos Client has the right to use. Client will cover Provider if a third party claims those materials were used without permission.

## 9. Who owns what

After **final payment**:

- Client owns the website content Client supplied and the look of the launched page for Client’s business.
- Client owns the domain.
- Provider may show the work in a portfolio unless Client asks in writing not to.
- Provider’s templates, code libraries, and tools stay Provider’s; Client gets a license to use them on this one site.

Until final payment, Provider owns the unpublished demo and code.

## 10. Limits

Provider is not liable for lost profits, search ranking, or how many customers the site brings in. Provider’s total liability under this agreement is the amount Client paid Provider in the last 12 months (here, up to $597 plus any care-plan fees).

Provider does not practice law, and this site is not legal, medical, or professional advice.

Either party may end the optional care plan with 7 days’ email notice. Launch fees already paid are not refunded after acceptance except under section 5.6.

## 11. Independent contractor

Provider is an independent contractor, not an employee or partner of Client.
